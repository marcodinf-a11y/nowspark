## How to interpret this

- For Germany, search German marks, EU marks and international registrations effective in Germany. DPMA warns that its search does not provide a similarity search and recommends EUIPO/WIPO sources for complete and current data. [DPMA search guidance](https://www.dpma.de/english/trade_marks/trade_mark_search/)
- A similar spelling, pronunciation or commercial impression can matter even without an exact match; relatedness of goods/services matters too. [USPTO likelihood-of-confusion guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion)
- Earlier unregistered use can also matter in the United States. A register-only search is incomplete. [USPTO comprehensive clearance guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks)
- WIPO recommends national/regional searches in addition to its own database. [WIPO Global Brand Database guidance](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database)
