# Alternative names: preliminary trademark and business-name sweep

Date: 2026-09-15. Scope: Start Riot, First Spark and NowSpark for an open-source Android procrastination/productivity app with desktop companion. Germany/EU and United States are priority search territories, not verified launch plans.

## Comparative result

| Candidate | Evidence found | Practical assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **First Spark** | Exact-name motivation app; Cyprus software company; related U.S. FIRSTSPARK software applications | Drop from shortlist: the motivation app overlaps the intended purpose directly |
| **Start Riot** | No exact-name commercial software brand identified in returned results; established RIOT software/mobile brands | Still needs similarity review; absence of an exact match does not resolve the RIOT component |
| **NowSpark** | No exact-name active app or relevant trademark record identified; historical UK company name; `.com` sale listing | Cleaner exact-name results in this limited sweep, not legal clearance |

## First Spark: concrete conflicts to investigate

1. **First Spark**, iPhone motivation app by **Rafael Melo de Farias**. Its indexed Austrian Apple App Store listing describes motivation tailored to low energy, wanting to quit, difficulty focusing, discipline and small next steps. This is close to the proposed purpose. Direct page fetches returned errors, so this finding rests on the search engine's indexed first-party listing; current storefront availability and launch date were not independently established. [Apple listing](https://apps.apple.com/at/app/first-spark/id6784022798)
2. **First Spark Technology Limited**, Larnaca, Cyprus, offers B2B software/platform development on its own website. The live page was retrieved. This confirms first-party software-brand use in an EU location, not registration or scope of trademark rights. [Company website](https://www.first-sparktech.com/)
3. **FIRSTSPARK GAMES**, U.S. serial **99198509**, owner FirstSpark Games Co., Ltd.; **FIRSTSPARK AI**, serial **99332201**, applicant Lifebook Acting Academy Inc. A retrieved **January 9, 2026 USPTO office action** for FIRSTSPARK AI explicitly identifies earlier FIRSTSPARK GAMES as a potential registration bar due to likelihood of confusion. The attachment records gaming/mobile software and software services in classes 9, 41 and 42 for FIRSTSPARK GAMES. This is a dated official record, **not verification of either application's current status** and not a ruling about the proposed app. [Official USPTO document](https://tmng-al.uspto.gov/resting2/api/casedoc/cms/case/99332201/office-action/OfficeAction8118759.pdf)
4. Another exact **First Spark** appears on Google Play as an Arizona GS mutant-recruitment game. This is less directly related than the motivation app, but confirms additional app-name crowding. [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.hackathon38.group2.firstspark)

## Start Riot: similar software brands

No relevant exact START RIOT or STARTRIOT brand was identified in returned results. However, **Riot Mobile** is a mobile companion offered by Riot Games, and **Riot** is also an employee cybersecurity software platform. These are similarity-review leads, not findings of infringement or claims that every name containing Riot is prohibited. [Riot Games announcement](https://playvalorant.com/en-gb/news/announcements/hello-riot-mobile-farewell-league/), [Riot cybersecurity website](https://nuxt.tryriot.com/)

## NowSpark: limited exact-name results

No relevant exact NOWSPARK trademark or active software product appeared in this sweep. The parallel company-register check confirmed company 02152357 used **NOWSPARK LIMITED** during August–December 1987, later became Magnum Securities Limited and was dissolved in 2017. The historical reference does not establish current use or rights in the proposed app category. [Companies House overview](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/02152357), [filing history](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/02152357/filing-history?page=4)

**NowSpark.com** is advertised for sale by HugeDomains; domain availability is separate from trademark rights. Price and registry checks belong to the parallel domain sweep. [Seller listing](https://www.hugedomains.com/domain_profile.cfm?d=nowspark.com)
