# Nowvolt naming sweep

Checked: 2026-09-15. Proposed use: open-source Android procrastination/productivity app with a desktop companion.

## Assessment

No obvious exact-name competing app emerged from this preliminary search. **Nowvolt is not legally cleared.** The .com domain is registered, a similar EU business name exists, and live trademark-register searches remain incomplete. Keep the app's final name undecided pending further checking.

## Search scope

General queries included `"Nowvolt"`, `"Now Volt"`, `"Nowvolt" trademark`, `"Nowvolt" app software`, `"nowvolt" company trademark brand`, `"now-volt" app`, `"nowvolt.com"`, and `"Nowvolt" domain`. Similar-name queries included `"Novolt" software app`, `"Novolt" software productivity`, `"Novolt" trademark`, and `"Nowvault" app`.

Site-restricted exact-name queries covered github.com, play.google.com (including /store/apps), apps.apple.com, and f-droid.org. Unrelated results and incidental occurrences of “now” followed by “Volt” were not treated as matching businesses.

## Practical next step

Complete live exact-and-similar trademark searches for the intended release countries before adopting the public brand. For Germany, DPMA recommends checking German, EU and relevant international rights, plus business names and other earlier signs. A targeted review by a trademark professional is the appropriate next step if legal confidence is the deciding factor. This sweep cannot predict whether someone will sue. [DPMA guidance](https://www.dpma.de/english/trade_marks/trade_mark_search/)
